📈 Good Years Summary (15 Years)

Years: 2000-2014, 2021

Average Growth: 9.7% | Median: 9.4%

Key Characteristics:

  • 2000-2007: Export-led boom, WTO accession benefits, infrastructure investment
  • 2008-2011: Massive stimulus package (¥4 trillion), maintained high growth despite global crisis
  • 2012-2014: Transition period with solid growth above 7.5%, "New Normal" begins
  • 2021: Strong post-COVID rebound with pent-up demand release

📉 Bad Years Summary (10 Years)

Years: 2015-2020, 2022-2024

Average Growth: 5.0% | Median: 5.1%

Key Characteristics:

  • 2015-2019: Structural slowdown, trade war impacts, growth declines from 7.0% to 6.1%
  • 2020: COVID-19 pandemic - worst year with only 2.3% growth
  • 2022: Property sector crisis, Zero-COVID policy impacts, 3.0% growth
  • 2023-2024: Weak recovery with structural headwinds (demographics, debt, property)

💡 Key Insights and Conclusions

1. Fundamental Transition

China has shifted from "super-growth" era (10%+) to "moderate-growth" era (5%). This is structural, not merely cyclical.

2. Cycle Length

Major cycles average 8-10 years, but recent cycles are shortening to 6-8 years due to external shocks and constraints.

3. Policy Effectiveness

Traditional stimulus tools (infrastructure, credit) becoming less effective. Structural reforms now essential.

4. Current Phase

China is in a challenging period (2022-2024) with growth around 3-5%, facing property crisis and weak consumption.
